## Governance

Governance is the mechanism by which ARENA holders collectively control the platform. It is **not operational at launch** — the team retains control during the initial growth phase — but the transition to full community governance is a core commitment.

### ✅ What Can Be Governed

- **League thresholds** — market cap cutoffs for Bronze / Silver / Gold / Diamond
- **Fight mechanics** — round duration, number of rounds, cooldown periods
- **Admission criteria** — minimum liquidity, volume, and pair age requirements
- **Matchmaking parameters** — market cap and volume similarity windows
- **Treasury allocation** — how platform revenue is deployed
- **Fee parameters** — prediction entry fees, platform rake percentages

### 🔒 What Cannot Be Governed (Immutable)

These are considered core platform safety guarantees and will not be subject to governance changes:

- The security requirement that mint and freeze authority **must be revoked** for any fighter
- The **anti-collusion rule** — tokens from the same deployer can never be matched
- **Integrity refund mechanics** — 78% refund on data-integrity failures protects predictors
