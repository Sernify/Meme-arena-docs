# The ARENA Token

ARENA is the upcoming native utility and governance token of the Meme Arena platform, built on Solana.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Status:** The ARENA Token is in pre-launch. The current platform operates entirely on the internal Points system. When the token launches, the official mint address and full details will be published here and аннотация via official channels.
{% endhint %}

***

## Why a Token?

The ARENA Token is designed to:

1. **Align incentives** — between the platform and its most active users
2. **Gate premium features** — holding ARENA unlocks capabilities beyond what Points provide
3. **Distribute governance** — token holders vote on platform parameters
4. **Capture platform value** — as Meme Arena grows, ARENA captures that growth

***

## Planned Utility at Launch

### ⚡ Prediction Boosts

Holding ARENA increases your Accuracy Pool share above the base rate. Larger ARENA holdings = higher multiplier on your proportional payout.

### 🏆 Exclusive Leagues

Certain tournament-style leagues will be gated to ARENA holders, featuring larger pools and higher-caliber fights.

### 📈 Increased Stake Limits

Non-token holders will have a maximum stake per prediction. ARENA holders will have higher (or no) stake limits, allowing larger positions.

### 💸 Entry Fee Reduction

The flat 15 pt entry fee per prediction may be reduced or waived for ARENA holders above certain thresholds.

### 🗳️ Governance

ARENA holders vote on platform parameters: league thresholds, fight durations, admission criteria, and treasury allocation.

***

## Points → ARENA

A portion of the ARENA token supply will be reserved for Points holders at the time of the snapshot. The exact conversion rate and eligibility criteria will be announced before the token launch.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Accumulate Points now.** Your prediction history and Points balance are being tracked and will matter at launch.
{% endhint %}

***

## Security Commitment

The ARENA token will launch with:

* ✅ **Mint authority revoked** at launch — no new supply can ever be created
* ✅ **Freeze authority revoked** — consistent with the same standards Meme Arena enforces for every fighter token
* ✅ LP locked for a defined period, published on-chain
* ✅ Team allocation subject to vesting with on-chain verification

Do not trust any unofficial "ARENA token" addresses until the official mint address is published here.
