***

## Fixed Supply

The ARENA token is **not inflationary** post-launch. Total supply is fixed at mint. Distribution happens from pre-allocated buckets — not from new minting.

***

## Platform Revenue Model

Meme Arena generates revenue through the **15 Points entry fee** per prediction. As the token matures, a portion of platform revenue may be:

* Distributed to staked ARENA holders
* Used for token buybacks and burns
* Allocated to the governance treasury

Specific mechanics will be voted on by ARENA holders post-launch.

***

## Liquidity Commitment

Initial liquidity will be seeded on a Solana DEX at launch. The team commits to:

* 🔒 Locking LP tokens for a defined period
* 📢 Publishing the LP lock transaction publicly
* 🗳️ Never pulling liquidity without a governance vote

***

## Vesting

Team and early allocations will be subject to vesting schedules with a cliff period. Full vesting schedules will be published at launch with on-chain verification links.
