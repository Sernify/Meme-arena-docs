# Placing Predictions

Making a prediction is how you put your market intuition to work. Here's the full flow.

---

## Before You Start

To place a prediction you need:

1. ✅ A **connected Solana wallet** (Phantom, Solflare, or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet)
2. ✅ A **Points balance** — new accounts receive **100 Points** on signup, no purchase required
3. ✅ An **open betting window** — predictions are only accepted during the 2-minute window before each match

---

## Step-by-Step

### 1. Find an Open Fight

From the **Arena** or **Fights** page, look for matches with an active betting window. The countdown timer shows exactly how long you have.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/screenshot-fights.jpg" alt="Fights page with live betting windows"></figure>

### 2. Choose Your Fighter

Each match shows two fighters side by side with their live stats:
- Current price and 24h change
- Market cap and 24h volume
- League and win/loss record

Pick the fighter you believe will outperform in the next 5 minutes.

### 3. Set Your Stake

Enter the number of Points you want to commit. Your maximum is your current Points balance minus the **15 pt entry fee**.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Entry Fee:** Each prediction carries a flat **15 Points** entry fee in addition to your stake. This fee goes to the platform and does not enter the Accuracy Pool.
{% endhint %}

### 4. Confirm

Review your prediction and confirm. Once submitted, your prediction is **locked** — you cannot change your fighter or adjust your stake.

---

## What Happens to Your Points

When you place a prediction:

```
Your Balance
    ├── Stake  ──→  Accuracy Pool (returned + profit if you win)
    └── 15 pts ──→  Platform fee (non-refundable)
```

---

## Prediction Outcomes

| Outcome | What Happens |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Win** | You receive your proportional share of the Accuracy Pool |
| ❌ **Loss** | Your stake goes to winning predictors |
| ⚠️ **Integrity Issue** | **78% refund** of your stake (fight couldn't be scored fairly) |

---

## Tips for Better Predictions

- **Watch volume momentum** before the window closes — volume tends to have inertia
- **Check the fighter profile** — some tokens have a strong win history in their league
- **League matters** — Diamond fights move slowly in % terms; Bronze can be wild
- **Pool size matters** — a small stake on a high-odds pool can outperform a large stake on a crowd favorite

{% hint style="warning" %}
You can only place **one prediction per match** per wallet. You must choose a side — there is no "draw" option.
{% endhint %}
