# Platform Roadmap

Meme Arena is an evolving platform. Here's where we've been, where we are, and where we're going.

***

## Current State — Live

* ✅ Real-time token discovery from DexScreener, PumpPortal, and PumpFun
* ✅ Automated security screening (mint/freeze authority, liquidity, volume gates)
* ✅ 4-league system (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond)
* ✅ Live 5-minute head-to-head fights
* ✅ Accuracy Pool — prediction rewards from real market performance
* ✅ Points economy with daily streaks and referral rewards
* ✅ Solana wallet authentication (Phantom, Solflare, and any WalletConnect-compatible wallet)
* ✅ Fighter profiles with on-chain metadata and AI-generated portrait art
* ✅ Anti-collusion matchmaking (deployer wallet checks)

***

## Near Term

* 🔨 **ARENA Token launch** — the native platform token (see [Tokenomics](../token/tokenomics.md))
* 🔨 **Token-gated features** — holding ARENA unlocks higher prediction limits, exclusive leagues, and boosted rewards
* 🔨 **Leaderboards** — global and per-league rankings for top predictors
* 🔨 **Tournament mode** — bracket-style elimination events with bigger prize pools
* 🔨 **Mobile-optimized experience** — native-feel UI improvements

## Medium Term

* 📋 **Governance** — ARENA holders vote on platform parameters (fight durations, league thresholds, admission criteria)
* 📋 **Multi-chain expansion** — extending the Arena to other chains beyond Solana
* 📋 **Social layer** — follow predictors, share fight highlights, prediction feeds
* 📋 **Advanced analytics** — fighter win/loss records, performance trends, historical matchups

## Long Term

* 🔮 **DAO transition** — full community ownership of matchmaking parameters and treasury
* 🔮 **SDK / API** — public data API for third-party integrations and bots
* 🔮 **Prediction markets expansion** — additional prediction types beyond head-to-head

***

> Roadmap items are subject to change based on community feedback and market conditions. Follow our socials for the latest updates.
