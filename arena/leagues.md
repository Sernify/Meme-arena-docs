# Leagues & Tiers

The Arena is divided into **four leagues**, each representing a different tier of meme token by market capitalization. Fighters are always matched within their own league — Bronze vs Bronze, Diamond vs Diamond.

### 🥉 Bronze League — Under $100,000

The proving grounds. This is where fresh Pump.fun graduates and newly discovered micro-cap meme tokens enter the Arena. Fights in Bronze can be chaotic — small market caps mean a single wallet can swing the result.

- **Typical fighters:** brand-new Pump.fun graduates, micro-cap memes
- **Market dynamic:** extremely volatile, high percentage swings
- **Best for:** high-risk, high-reward predictions

---

### 🥈 Silver League — $100K to $1M

Silver fighters have survived the early grind and built a real community. More stable than Bronze, but still capable of violent moves. Most of the Arena's activity lives here.

- **Typical fighters:** established micro-caps with active communities
- **Market dynamic:** volatile but with more liquidity depth
- **Best for:** balanced risk-reward predictions

---

### 🥇 Gold League — $1M to $10M

Gold is the mid-card of the Arena. These tokens are well-known in the meme ecosystem, have significant liquidity, and attract larger trading volumes.

- **Typical fighters:** recognized meme tokens with sustained traction
- **Market dynamic:** more measured moves, stronger volume
- **Best for:** trend-based predictions

---

### 💎 Diamond League — Over $10M

The main event. Diamond fighters are the household names of meme culture — tokens with massive communities, deep liquidity, and real market influence.

- **Typical fighters:** top-tier Solana memes, blue-chip degeneracy
- **Market dynamic:** slower percentage moves, but massive absolute volume
- **Best for:** informed predictions based on ecosystem momentum

---

## League Transitions

League assignment is **automatic and continuous**. The platform re-evaluates every token's market cap every 30 seconds. If a token crosses a threshold, it is reassigned immediately.

```
Bronze → Silver   when MCap crosses $100,000
Silver → Gold     when MCap crosses $1,000,000
Gold   → Diamond  when MCap crosses $10,000,000
(and downward in reverse)
```

{% hint style="info" %}
A token in the middle of a scheduled fight completes it in its current league, then is reassigned for subsequent matchmaking.
{% endhint %}

---

## Why Leagues Matter for Predictions

Fights only occur between tokens **in the same league**. This is important because:

1. **Similar volatility profiles** — comparing apples to apples
2. **Similar liquidity depth** — no whale advantage from market cap mismatch
3. **Tight matchmaking** — within-20% market cap requirement keeps fights competitive
