# Daily Rewards & Streaks

Log in every day to claim your daily bonus. The longer you maintain your streak, the bigger your rewards.

---

## How to Claim

1. Go to your **Profile** page
2. If your daily bonus is available, the **Claim** button will be active
3. Click it — Points are instantly added to your balance

The claim button shows a countdown timer when you're in cooldown, so you always know exactly when your next bonus is ready.

---

## Cooldown

The daily bonus has a **20-hour cooldown**, not 24 hours. This means you can claim slightly earlier each day. Over a week, this lets you naturally shift your claim time without missing a day.

> If you miss a day and let the cooldown lapse past 24 hours, your **streak resets to 1**.

---

## Streak Bonuses

Consecutive daily claims build your streak counter. Higher streaks unlock higher bonuses:

- **Day 1** — standard daily bonus
- **Longer streaks** — progressively higher bonuses (exact values shown in-app)

Your current streak and the next streak milestone are displayed on your Profile page.

---

## Streak Reset

Your streak resets to 1 if more than ~24 hours pass since your last claim. The 20-hour cooldown gives you a 4-hour grace window each day, but missing it entirely resets progress.

---

## Why Daily Rewards Exist

Daily rewards serve two purposes:
1. Reward consistent platform participation
2. Give new users a way to build a Points balance even before they make their first prediction
